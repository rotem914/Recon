# Visual QA pass, 2026-10-06

The first pass of `project-os/Visual_QA.md` on Recon: the editor used, not read. Scope: the
screens the work since 2026-09-27 renders, and their neighbours. The stage with its callouts,
the ruler and the crop; the timeline with its tabs, thumbnails, dropdown, done marks, reorder
and delete; the Settings modal; the sidebar, the window buttons and the size band. Nothing
in the repository was changed by this pass but this document, its History rows and one
paragraph of method in `project-os/Visual_QA.md`.

Verdict vocabulary per finding: **fix / drop / backlog**. Every finding here is pre-existing:
none was introduced by a change under review. Rotem's verdicts of 2026-10-06: T1 drop, T2 fix,
T3 fix, both fixed the same day (`editor/index.html`, with a check each in `editor-checks.js`);
the nits T4 to T8 wait for his word.

## How it was driven

The checks build (`cargo build --features stage0-checks`) opened its demo window on the demo's
own store beside the executable, never Rotem's library, and was driven through WebView2's
debugging port with real mouse and key events (`.tmp/visual-qa/probe.mjs`, `probe2.mjs`,
`probe3.mjs`; their logs and screenshots sit beside them). Three rounds, 1280 by 800 CSS
pixels at a 2.25 ratio, the console watched throughout: no error and no warning in any round.
The demo's store, tabs and settings were left as found: empty.

Two limits, named so the result can be read right:

- The display was locked or asleep for the whole pass (a screen copy fails with an invalid
  handle). The demo's own start-up screenshots its window and ends the run when that fails,
  so a script on the page's Tauri bridge answered the demo and checks flags with "no" before
  the page booted; the product page then ran as it does in the product. Screenshots are the
  renderer's, not the real screen's, so the first-frame check of the always-look-for list
  did not run.
- Nothing that writes the clipboard was pressed: no Ctrl+C, no Copy and Return, no colour
  picker click. It is Rotem's clipboard, and his Recon was running.

## What held

Named so the next pass need not repeat them, and so a green here can be trusted: each line
says what would have turned it red.

- A note of three lines typed by key, English, Hebrew, English, came back as typed with its
  two breaks, each line aligned by its own language (left 0 / right 0 / left 0 px), the same
  after a save and a store reload, and document.json holds the text verbatim.
- Ctrl+Z while typing takes the typing back and keeps the note; Ctrl+Z after Escape takes the
  note; Ctrl+Shift+Z brings it back where it was.
- A thousand characters with no space wrap inside the bubble (48 lines, no overflow) and
  grow the margin below the picture; emoji and mixed-direction text come back whole. Delete
  with the bubble selected, Ctrl+Z brings it back under its own number, Ctrl+Shift+Z deletes.
- A bubble left with spaces only goes and gives its number back, with no step of history.
- Ctrl+click marks thumbnails with the one on screen, a second Ctrl+click unmarks; the right
  press opens Remove from tab above Delete; Escape, a click outside and Enter each close it,
  the window staying; ArrowDown goes round; Remove from tab takes the batch out and one
  Ctrl+Z puts every picture back in its place.
- The done mark shows under the pointer, a press ticks it and keeps it shown, Ctrl+Z clears it.
- A batch deleted with the picture on screen in it: two in the trash, the neighbour shown, one
  Ctrl+Z brings both back with the done mark kept and the trash at 0. Ctrl+Delete the same.
- A tab deleted by its menu: its two pictures stay in Main, Ctrl+Z brings it back byte for
  byte, name, order and done mark, and tabs.json follows. A right press on Main opens nothing.
- A thumbnail dragged to the first place in a tab lands there, tabs.json holds the order,
  Ctrl+Z puts it back. A whitespace rename keeps the old name; Ctrl+Z undoes a rename.
- Settings opens by the chevron and closes by Escape, by the veil and by its X; Ctrl+Z inside
  reaches no note; the checkbox persists to the settings file and comes back after a reopen;
  two fast presses end where they began; a shortcut field waits, refuses a bare key in words,
  and Escape stops the wait with the modal still open. Opening Settings commits a note being typed.
- A tool button paints #21222C with the icon line #E7E8E9 under the pointer; its tooltip says
  its name; Enter and Space on a focused button pick the tool; Escape puts it down with the
  window staying.
- The ruler draws by a real drag with "200x120" beside it and the zoom circle under the
  pointer, its x deletes it, Ctrl+Z brings it back. A crop handle dragged cuts to 103x200, the
  size band follows, Ctrl+Z restores 440x200. The wheel zooms 1 to 1.25 and back to the fit.
